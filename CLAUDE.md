# kecman.co

Filip's personal site and security blog. Static HTML, no build step. Blog posts live in `blog/*.html`, listed on `blog.html`, and registered in `sitemap.xml`.

## Blog post writing rules

- **No em dashes, no en dashes, and no double-hyphen (`--`) dash substitutes anywhere in post content, titles, or meta descriptions.** Rewrite the sentence instead: split into two sentences, or use a comma, colon, semicolon, or parentheses. This applies to visible body text as well as `<title>`, meta description, OG tags, and JSON-LD fields.
- Hyphens inside normal compound words (e.g. `well-known`, `non-exported`, `state-of-the-art`) are fine — this rule is about the dash used as sentence-connecting punctuation, not word-forming hyphens.
